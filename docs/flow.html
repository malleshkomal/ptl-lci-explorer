Reading the diagram

Line width follows the square root of the amount. Electricity and mass use separate scales.

  • Dashed lines out to “Water” are not in the dataset. They are the mass balance left over at each step (inputs minus product).
  • The second line in each box is the equipment share: how much product one unit of plant or equipment makes over its lifetime.
  • These are inventory quantities, not impact scores. Background database: ecoinvent 3.9 cut-off.
